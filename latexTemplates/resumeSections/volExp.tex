\resumeSubheading{Director Learning Assistant Program}{5/2017-6/2022}
{East Carolina University} {Greenville, NC}

\resumeItemListStart

\resumeItem{Founded and directed the East Carolina University Learning Assistant Program
  (ECU LA Program) to answer the challenge to create more science majors at ECU. The LA
  program is a faculty-driven program designed to improve outcomes in introductory STEM
  courses by integrating advanced students into these courses as peer leaders. As
  director, I recruited both faculty and students to participate in this program,
  developed and implemented pedagogical training for both faculty and
  students. Additionally, I managed the program's budget (approx. \$200k/year) and
  integrated the ECU LA program into the International LA program community. I also
  designed and implemented program evaluation practices for the LA program and reported
  outcomes from the program to university stakeholders such as department chairs, deans,
  and the university provost.}

\resumeItem{During my tenure, the ECU LA Program supported 142 LAs in 55 classes with 31
  faculty and reached over 3000 students.}

\resumeSubheading{Editor Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings}{5/2018 -
    12/2020} {American Association of Physics Teachers}{College Park, MD}

  \resumeItemListStart

  \resumeItem{I served a 3-year term with the Physics Education Research Conference
    Proceedings, holding the title \textit{Assistant Editor} in 2018, \textit{Editor} in 2019,
    and \textit{Editor-in-Chief} in 2020. The Physics Education Conference is the largest
    conference for the Physics Education Research field, and has approximately 500 attendees
    each year. In each of the years that I served in this capacity, we had over 140 papers
    submitted for peer review. In addition to standard editorial duties such as reviewing
    scientific papers to ensure quality and accuracy, my tenure on the editorial team saw me
    take the lead in several changes leading to the improvement of the proceedings, especially
    in the area of improving equity and access.}

  \resumeItem{While serving as \textit{Editor} the Proceedings began a transition from single
    confidential review to double confidential review. I identified aspects of the current
    timeline that needed to be addressed, and I created and distributed a new \LaTeX\ template
    for prospective proceedings authors to utilize in order to adhere to the new requirement
    for double confidential review.}

  \resumeItem{While serving as \textit{Editor-in-chief} I worked to formalize the appeal
    process for authors to appeal editorial decisions. Previous to my time as an editor,
    well-connected authors would contact editors, and appeal their decision. But this
    ``back-channel'' method was not publicized or formalized at all. I communicated with the
    community about the existence of the back-channel appeal process and laid out guidelines
    about what would be considered appropriate grounds for an appeal.}
