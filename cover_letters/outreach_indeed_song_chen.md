## Why this framing

**The sent note**
- **The transition is the hook, not an aside.** Song Chen left a math professorship for industry, so Steven is writing from the same starting point rather than asking for a favor. That is a real point of contact.
- **The ask is framed as learning, not crediting.** The note says "I would value connecting... as well as learn about your experience transitioning into industry" rather than citing ADPList or MentorCruise. Asking a stranger to describe his own path is a normal request; telling a stranger you have read his mentoring profile is presumptuous. Dropping the mentoring citation also removes the risk of getting his history slightly wrong.
- **The role is named first.** The note opens with the position, so the connection request is legible in a notification preview even if the transition angle is what makes it worth accepting.
- **"That you posted recently" is an inference, not a verified fact.** The posting names Song Chen as hiring manager, but iCIMS reqs are often submitted by a recruiter on the manager's behalf. The posting date of 2026-10-21 makes "recently" safe. If he replies saying a team posted it, the misattribution is minor and self-correcting.

**The DM (held until accepted)**
- **It continues rather than repeats.** The note already covered the transition, so the DM opens on the role and on what specifically overlaps with his work.
- **Executive decisions** is Song Chen's own stated focus ("translating complex analyses into tools that inform executive decisions"), and it is the same thread as the reworked cover-letter bullet: pilot-period experimental results driving a $500K+ permanent funding decision, reported to deans and the university provost.
- **R package** mirrors Song's standardized R package for LLM pipelines with Steven's concorR. Both are real, shipped, open artifacts, not aspirations.
- **The gap is stated up front** (no production experimentation infrastructure, industry exposure limited to AI-lab work directing opencode). Volunteering it is more credible than letting the recruiter find it, and it fits a physicist's norm of reporting what the evidence supports.
